Overview

Lead anchor Case Blackwell presides over a chaotic Friday broadcast covering the OpenAI Codex Micro keyboard reveal, a Biden family book promo, Pete Hegseth’s testosterone screening program, Russian robot weddings, and a Foundation Robotics humanoid weapons platform. The broadcast features a degenerate Hype Train interrupt, a Munch vs Crum merch debate that reignites the Crum Dick Punch rivalry, a Jeb & Dad instructional triple-feature, a Therapy session with Dr. Chath, and concludes with DJ C4 anchoring the DJC4 studios and producing a disturbing Stephanie-themed merch-drop rap with AI assistance for Crum to perform.
