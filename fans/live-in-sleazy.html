Live ‘n Sleazy

Live ‘n Sleazy (usually heard as “Live in Sleazy” or “Live and Sleazy”) is one of Case’s two “master mods,” alongside Sabreche. They’ve been in chat since at least November 2023 (“Live and Sleazy, the other master mod… The best mod in the business is both of them”) and are named on air in about 250 broadcasts through November 2026. They’re a viewer, not a character, so this page is filed under Fanatical Fans rather than On-Air Talent.

Sleazy is the chatter Case trusts on facts. He regularly takes their word over his own (“if it’s from Live in Sleazy it is always a real answer”), tracks down the clip or article he’s fumbling for, and jokes that they know where things are in his house better than he does. They’re also the most frequent source of Amongst the Web submissions, which Case treats as a gamble. Off the news desk, they handle mod chores like making Discord threads and running polls, helped Case spec and build his streaming PC, send Brandon the games and ROMs he plays, and have made assets and AI videos for the show. They request segments (a Munch & Crum debate, How To with Jeb, the rarely run AI Rooney) and songs for DJ C4. From October 2025 on, AI Rooney opens a long run of rants with “Live and Sleazy got me thinking,” covering condom sizes (2025-11-27), pythons going for your grundle (2026-04-24), vapes (2026-04-27), $120 hot dogs (2026-05-04), shaving (2026-05-12) and soup that’s too spicy (2026-06-22).

The Live in Sleazy Dice

Because so many Amongst the Web clips come from Sleazy, and they range from great to deliberately awful, Case started introducing them as a roll of the dice. “Let’s end with a submission from Live and Sleazy. Meaning it could be incredible, or we could intentionally end on a terrible one… It’s all up to the whims of Live and Sleazy” (2025-08-02). Later versions: “he might punish us or reward us. It’s really at Live in Sleazy’s discretion” (2025-12-04); “Live and Sleazy is a knife or a massage, and you never know what you’ll get” (2025-12-09); “It’s like a Targaryen being born or whatever. The world sees a new clip from Live in Sleazy and holds their breath” (2025-12-18). Sleazy has become the yardstick: fellow chatter Psychor is “almost a Live and Sleazy level savant when it comes to rolling the dice on video quality” (2026-01-27).

The usual punishment for a bad clip, “ban the submitter,” doesn’t work on a mod. On 2025-01-15, after Sleazy posted a Marjorie Taylor Greene video that chat rated a zero, Case ran a poll on whether Sleazy should ban themselves. Chat filled in the options, and “self suck” won: “Live in Sleazy, we’ll give you a couple months to take the necessary yoga classes, but that is your punishment for posting that Marjorie Taylor Greene video.” By 2025-04-04 the rule was settled: “you can only get Live and Sleazy to ban themselves. Or you have to make Live and Sleazy and Sabreche go to war with each other. Nobody wants that.”

Mod Work and Tech Help

Case relies on Sleazy for the parts of running a stream he doesn’t understand. When he asked whether they could make threads in the Discord, “Live and Sleazy is the hero we need and he’s already made it” (2025-05-13). They sent him his channel analytics (2025-05-27), suggested the timed news roundups with Peter (2025-03-04), and made the assets for the Papal Binclave, “saving me honestly several hours” (2025-05-05). For hardware, Case built his PC “mostly begging Live and Sleazy and other members of the Cream Team to tell me how to do it” (2025-04-25), bought “all the RAM that Live and Sleazy told me to get” (2025-11-18), and later said “Live and Sleazy basically built it for me” (2026-06-22). On game streams, they fill the gaming thread and send the ROMs, with emulator instructions Brandon often can’t follow: “Thanks to Live and Sleazy for always providing the ROMs, even though Brandon is not smart and doesn’t get how to load them” (2026-05-02).
