Skymer

Skymer (skymyr on Twitch) has been in Case’s chat since at least November 2023 and is named on air in more than 200 broadcasts. They’re a viewer, not a character, so this page is filed under Fanatical Fans rather than On-Air Talent.

Skymer is a long-time subscriber (Case read out 35 months in October 2025 and 36 in May 2026), a steady source of 100-, 300- and 1,000-bit cheers, and a regular in Case’s opening roll calls and sign-off raps. Their bits have summoned the presidential bit alert about 20 times, and their chat lines get read on the desk, turned into AI Rooney complaints, and used against Crum. They once called in live to Guy Redacted, and in May 2026 Case made them a VIP. Even after all that, nobody on the show can agree on how to say the name.

Summoning the President

A 300-bit cheer sets off an alert in Joe Biden’s voice: “Listen up, Jack. Skymer just mama-jammed 300 shiny tasty coins, and I’ve been dying to say this,” followed by whatever the president has to say. At 1,000 bits Barack Obama turns up instead: “Skymer just absolutely pounded 1,000 sweet bits straight into the great chasm of progress.” Skymer has set these off around 20 times since May 2024:

  • 2024-07-15, six days before the real Biden dropped out: “I’m stepping aside so Hype Train can be our new nominee.” (“I graciously accept. I’m now running for president.“)
  • 2025-06-11, Case’s birthday: Biden: “Happy birthday, young man. I remember when I was your age.” Later, Obama: “I’m already in SEAL Team 6 to take out Crum.”
  • 2025-12-18: While Case is struggling to get the Hype Train to appear, Biden turns up to plug “my sweet plaque at the White House.” Case: “Thank you so much, Mr. President, for your kind words, and Skymer for summoning the president. Today we are all Skymer.”
  • 2026-03-07: In the middle of Crum’s mock trial, Obama arrives on Skymer’s 1,000 bits: “Ladies and gentlemen, we got him. Our long national nightmare is over. Fuck Crum.”
  • 2026-07-24: Obama brings back Obamacare to treat Case’s ankle injury, though because of budget cuts it’s “mostly a thoughts and prayers based treatment.” Case: “Skymer, far too kind with the thousand bits that’ll go straight into the thoughts and prayers fund for my ankle.”

Shut Up, Crum

Skymer’s messages are a reliable source of material against Crum, and Munch reads them out gladly. “Yes, gross Crum. Thank you, Skymer” (2024-03-01). Skymer suggested the insult “J.K. Crum rolling,” and Crum himself admitted it was “more fun to say” (2024-08-01). Munch thanked Skymer for apologizing “for anyone having to see Crum” (2025-02-12), and in a debate over baby photos agreed with their theory that Crum “was in a cocoon from most of his childhood” (“That sounds right, Skymer,” 2025-12-09). While Crum was supposed to be awaiting his dick punch, chat, “and particularly Skymer,” wanted to know: “Why aren’t you dead yet, Crum?” (2026-03-19, see Crum Dick Punch). More recently: “Point is, shut up, Crum. Skymer gets it” (2026-07-13); “Yes, absolutely, Skymer, give him Havana syndrome” (2026-09-01); and at skeleton Munch’s Rennerton press conference, a “great point, Skymer” about how to stop future Crum attacks: “the answer is 100% execute Crum” (2026-11-06).
