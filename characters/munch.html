Munch (Ralph Munchcut)

Ralph Munchcut (known on air simply as Munch) is a senior debate analyst on Barely Informed News, anchoring the network’s premier political debate clash, Munch & Crum.

Overview

Munch serves as the combative, unapologetic political counterweight to Crum on Barely Informed News. Distinguished by his disheveled silver mane, Munch brings relentless swagger and hostility to the debate desk.

On 2024-05-15, Crum’s first debate-score victory after 200+ losses broke Munch into the soft alter ego Al Manchester (debuted as Ale Chrome Chester / Ale Munchester). The nice-guy shell lasted through early June; by mid-June Munch was restored (hair back, ego intact), with Crum insisting Al remains trapped inside.

Their volatile dynamic also forms the backbone of the Crum Punch trilogy: Munch goaded the 2025 face-punch Crumsplosion, then rescued Crum from hell after weeks of volleyball-corpse denial, and finally agitated for the 2026 groin-punch sequel. On September 7, 2026, the punch backfired: Crum went “demon mode” in his hell form, and the fireball burned Munch down to an immortal talking skeleton (see Skeleton Munch).

Skeleton Munch

Since 2026-09-07, Munch has been a living, talking skeleton. It’s a new manifestation of Munch himself, not an alter ego and not Al Manchester.

  • Origin. When the Rock-afire Gorilla finally threw the long-awaited Crum Dick Punch, the stage erupted in “a shit load of fire” [1:50:40]. Munch, standing by to gloat, took the blast; Crum walked away unscathed. Munch’s verdict: “You went all fucking demon mode” [1:51:55]. “You turn me into a skeleton?” he demands; Crum calls that “a little bit of an exaggeration… there was an accident” [1:56:24].
  • Crum’s defense. Crum calls it Munch’s “incredibly self-imposed condition” and says “even in my hell form my intention was [not] for you to become a talking skeleton,” blaming Munch for “absorbing a fireball” (2026-11-03 [54:25–54:51]). Munch: “you still tried to make me one.”
  • Embracing it. Munch insists he “meant to be a skeleton,” that it shows off his superior bone structure, and that “skeletons are so in right now.” He calls himself a “skelly,” bills himself Skellomunch (2026-10-22), and claims the title Lord of the Dead with a summonable penis (2026-09-25). He bristles at assumptions about his “skeletal identity” (2026-10-19).
  • Green level-up. During the Rebuild Rennerton Valheim stream he turns green after combat (2026-10-23), later explaining he “leveled up” by killing a spirit to progress his tech tree (2026-10-26).
  • Status. Permanent so far: he debates, mayors Rennerton, and haunts the desk as a skeleton.

Canon notes

  • Since 2026-09-07 Munch is a permanent talking skeleton (“Skeleton Munch”, “Skellomunch”, “skelly”), burned down by Crum’s demon/hell-form fireball when the Crum Dick Punch backfired. Tag skeleton beats to Crum Dick Punch or Munch, never to Al Manchester.
  • Al Manchester is only the bald, soft-spoken alter ego from the 2024-05-15 scoreboard breakdown (concluded June 2024). Skeleton Munch is not Al Manchester.
