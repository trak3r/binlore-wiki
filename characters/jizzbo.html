Jizzbo

Jizzbo (occasionally misheard in archive notes as Jizbo or Gizbo) is a recurring Barely Informed News persona — wall electrician / maintenance dwarf, guest vocalist, and later a mock-trial witness who answers examination with a conga.

Overview & Persona

Jizzbo first appears during Join the Wall as the studio electrician who descends into the drywall with Munch, later returns as a DJ C4 guest artist, and is woven into Crum marital lore as “Freddie Jizzbo.” During the 2026-03-07 peen.ui IP mock trial he is called back from the mountain, announces “I’m here. It’s me. It’s jizzbo,” claims to still be in great shape, and meets a defense prompt by launching into Gloria Estefan’s “Conga” instead of testimony.

The court sustains the musical interruption despite Jizzbo clarifying he does not believe a question was asked.
